# Press and critiques

**TechCrunch (2026-05-20)** — confirms launch date, 459M-2.7B range, Warner/Universal licensing; competitive context: Google Lyria 3 Pro, ElevenLabs music, Suno/Udio (both in training-data litigation); Stability hiring music-industry veterans (Ethan Kaplan).

**Dubspot review** (https://blog.dubspot.com/stable-audio-3-review) — strongest contrarian source:
- "None of the models do vocals or lyrics" — biggest editorial decision; use Suno/Udio for vocal tracks.
- "Song structure and progression are weaker than what the competition delivers."
- "The same prompt across multiple seeds often produces near-identical melodies with slightly different decorations."
- "The music can feel correct without feeling interesting." Rhythmic genres flatten rather than surprise.
- Net for LoopMaster: instrumental/SFX focus actually FITS a loop/one-shot tool; the weaknesses hit song-length composition, not loops.

**AIToolTier** (https://aitooltier.com/tools/stable-audio, low-medium confidence) — no third-party Suno v5/Udio benchmarks at launch; "open-weight" narrative undercut by API-only 2.7B Large.
