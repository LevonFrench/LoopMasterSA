# Stability DAW plugin (confirmed)

- Announced **2026-08-18** (Louisa Marshall, official blog). Early beta.
- Plugin: **AU + VST3, macOS only** (Logic Pro, Ableton Live; Apple Silicon + Intel). No Windows, no AAX/CLAP mentioned.
- Inference appears **cloud-based**; no local/offline claim made.
- Features: generate on a DAW track, tempo sync to session BPM, length up to 6-min, multi-take playlists.
- StableAudio.com upgrade: audio-to-audio iteration, per-track mixing (level/pan/mute/solo/FX), master effects, export.
- Pricing: **not disclosed**. No independent press coverage found yet (1 day old).
- Relevance: first-party tool does NOT cover Windows or local inference — LoopMaster's niche (local, Windows, loops/kits) remains uncontested by Stability's own tooling.
