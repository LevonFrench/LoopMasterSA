# Meet Stable Audio 3

- Released **May 20, 2026** (TechCrunch corroborates; one agent fetch mis-extracted 2025).
- Four variants: **Small SFX** (mobile/laptop SFX), **Small** (on-device music), **Medium** (up to 6:20 tracks), **Large** (API/enterprise only, not open-weight).
- Small SFX / Small / Medium ship open weights on HuggingFace; Large does not.
- Novel **semantic-acoustic autoencoder (SAME)**; LoRA fine-tuning supported for Small and Medium; inpainting, multi-segment editing, causal continuation.
- Trained on fully licensed data via Warner Music Group and Universal Music Group partnerships (positioned against Suno/Udio litigation exposure).
- **Stability AI Community License**: free, own your outputs, distribute/commercialize (under $1M annual revenue); Enterprise License above that, with indemnification.
