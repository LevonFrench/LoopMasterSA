# Quick Start Guide

1. Use the random buttons to cook up variations in key. Use the instrument buttons to fill out what your aranggement is missing.
2. Set your BPM, Steps (higher takes longer to generate), and press Generate to start.
3. Playback should start automatically! 2x and 4x will extend loops you like. Delete and regen the ones you don't. All loops are ACiDized and ready to drop in your own DAW. You can also export a zip of the currently playing loops or render a full mix down to a wav file from the export panel.
4. FX & Modulation are WIP. The macro knobs can add some quick color and bring the mix together. There's limiting, compression and prompt massaging working behind the scenes
