Welcome to LoopMaster SA3

Generate dynamic multi-track audio loops using generative AI.
Control playback, mixing, and generation instantly via keyboard shortcuts.

Quick Start
1

Choose curated values in each prompt section, type a custom override, or use Randomize All. The dice beside each section rerolls only that value.

2

Set your BPM, Steps (higher takes longer to generate), and press Generate [G] to start.

3

Playback starts automatically! 2x and 4x extend loops you like; delete and regen the ones you don't. All loops are ACiDized and ready to drop into your DAW. You can also export a ZIP or render a full mixdown from the export panel.

4

FX & Modulation are WIP. The macro knobs add quick color and bring the mix together. Limiting, compression, and prompt massaging work behind the scenes.
